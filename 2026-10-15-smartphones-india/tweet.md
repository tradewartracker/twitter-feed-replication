## Post 2 (reply)

Link: https://x.com/tradewartracker/status/2110740503473918135

> The #tariff treatment tells the story.
>
> Smartphones were exempt from Liberation Day, but NOT from the fentanyl tariff on China. So:
>
> - phones from China paid ~20% through 2025 (10% from Nov with the Busan truce)
>
> - phones from India and Vietnam paid ~0
>
> The fentanyl tariff is gone since March 2026, but the shift to India has not reversed.
